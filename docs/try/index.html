Measuring a voice needs Baseline Drift running on your own computer, which is also what keeps recordings private. This page shows the sample report only. To record your own voice, follow the steps at https://github.com/acmross/baseline-drift.

  1. 1

    Sustained "ah" about 5 seconds

    Take a normal breath and hold 'ah' at a comfortable, steady pitch and volume until you run out of comfortable breath.

    Not recorded
  2. 2

    Fixed passage up to 30 seconds

    Read this aloud at your own pace. There is no need to rush. The text appears when this take begins.

    Commander's log, day forty. Sixteen sunrises today and I just caught three of them. The coffee floats and the plants are leaning toward the window again. We flushed the pump, checked the hatch seals, and measured the garden. The ship is in one piece and the stars are where we left them this evening. Signing off until tomorrow.
    Not recorded
  3. 3

    Free speech 30 seconds

    Talk through these in your own words. There are no wrong answers.

    • How did you sleep?
    • Any cold, congestion, sore throat or coughing?
    • Any unusual pain or discomfort?
    • Anything else about how you are doing today?
    Not recorded
