We flushed the pump, checked the hatch seals, and measured the garden. The ship is in one piece and the stars are where we left them this evening. Signing off until tomorrow.

    Free speech 30 seconds

    Talk through these in your own words. There are no wrong answers.

    • How did you sleep?
    • Any cold, congestion, sore throat or coughing?
    • Any unusual pain or discomfort?
    • Anything else about how you are doing today?
